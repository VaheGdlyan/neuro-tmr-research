## BOAS Metadata Due Diligence

* Confirmed the recording architecture:

  * **128 recording nights**;
  * `sub-*` represents a recording/night;
  * `pid` represents the real participant;
  * the current `participants.tsv` contains **100 unique `pid`s**.
* Identified an unresolved documentation inconsistency: the BOAS README states **108 unique individuals**, while the current local `participants.tsv` contains **100 unique `pid`s**.
* Confirmed that every BOAS night contains simultaneously recorded **clinical PSG** and **Bitbrain wearable headband** data.
* Confirmed the core dataset-wide availability across all 128 nights:

  * full six-channel PSG EEG;
  * PSG EOG and EMG;
  * both wearable EEG channels (`HB_1`, `HB_2`);
  * matching PSG/headband recording durations;
  * matching PSG/headband sleep-stage epoch counts.
* Confirmed that the wearable EEG channels are approximately located at **AF7 / AF8** and that additional IMU and PPG channels are available for many nights.
* Confirmed that sleep staging is represented in aligned **30-second epochs**.
* Decoded the BOAS staging labels:

  * `0` — Wake
  * `1` — N1
  * `2` — N2
  * `3` — N3
  * `4` — REM
  * `8` — PSG disconnection
  * `-2` — AI artifact / missing data
* Confirmed that `stage_hum` is derived from **PSG-based expert consensus scoring**, using three independent expert scorers with a fourth scorer resolving complete disagreement.
* Confirmed that `stage_ai` represents separate Bitbrain automatic staging for PSG and wearable-headband recordings.
