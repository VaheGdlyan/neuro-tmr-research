# 2026-10-27 — PV-3 Held-Out Expert-Label Evaluation

## Objective

Evaluate the existing PSG-only causal replay pipeline and N3 persistence
controller against expert sleep-stage labels across held-out participants.

## Work completed

- Prepared and tested the held-out evaluation workflow, preserving the saved
  model, controller rules, and separation between replay and expert labels.
- Evaluated 25 recordings from 20 participants excluded from model training.
  Two previously verified recordings were reused; 23 were newly replayed.
- All recordings completed and passed the independent software verifier.
  The evaluation included 23,745 complete windows; 23,728 passed the existing
  signal-validity checks.
- Ran a retrospective transition analysis around dummy triggers using saved
  outputs. No model or controller tuning was performed.

## Main results

- N3 classifier: precision 1,010/2,692 (37.52%); recall 1,010/1,098 (91.99%).
- ALLOWED windows labelled N3: 954/2,118 (45.04%).
- Expert-N3 windows ALLOWED: 954/1,098 (86.89%).
- Trigger-associated windows labelled N3: 41/238 (17.23%). Of 239 total
  triggers, one had an unusable code-8 reference label.
- Of 196 triggers associated with expert-labelled N2, 31 (15.82%) were
  followed by an observed N3 label within the next five 30-second windows;
  165 were not.

## Interpretation and boundary

The pipeline ran across the held-out group, but many predicted-N3 windows and
controller entries were labelled N2 by the experts. The evaluation used
recorded BOAS data and dummy events. Completed-window labels do not establish
the exact sleep state at the end-of-window trigger timestamp.

These are within-BOAS held-out results, not external validation, live audio
delivery, or evidence of TMR efficacy.
