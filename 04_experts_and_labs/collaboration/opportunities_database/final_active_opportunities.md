## Purpose

This file is the main operational database for Path E collaboration work after L8.

It contains only opportunities that received the final L8 decision:

> **`KEEP`**

These 53 opportunities form the active-first collaboration portfolio.

`RESERVE`, `DEFER`, and `DROP` opportunities are intentionally excluded from this file.

- `RESERVE` opportunities remain preserved in `l8_ranked_opportunities.md` for selective later activation.
- `DEFER` and `DROP` opportunities remain preserved in `l8_scoring_ledger.md` for full traceability.

This document does **not** re-score or re-rank candidates. It inherits the completed L8 decisions and rankings.

## Operating Principle

> **Work first from the active KEEP portfolio. Activate RESERVE opportunities only when the active portfolio produces insufficient traction, leaves a required capability uncovered, or new evidence materially changes the comparative picture.**

The next Path E layers may determine sequencing, outreach waves, dependencies, contact strategy, and messaging, but should not rewrite the completed L8 evaluation unless a documented factual error is found.
