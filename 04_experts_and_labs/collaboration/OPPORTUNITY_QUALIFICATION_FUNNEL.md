## 1. Purpose

The Opportunity Universe contains:

**OPP-001 → OPP-167**

across 12 discovery sections.

Discovery was intentionally broad and inclusive. Inclusion in the database does **not** mean that an opportunity is:

- verified,
- current,
- accessible,
- relevant enough,
- high quality,
- strategically valuable,
- or worth acting on.

The purpose of this funnel is to convert the raw discovery universe into a smaller, evidence-checked and actionable opportunity portfolio.

The governing transition is:

> **Discovery was intentionally inclusive. Qualification must be intentionally strict.**

The funnel must answer:

> **Which of the 167 discovered opportunities are real, current, distinct, accessible, relevant, high-quality, strategically useful, and worth acting on?**

Only after that question is answered may the Path E 80/20 rule be applied.

# 2. Core Principles

## 2.1 Discovery Freeze

The discovery universe is frozen at:

**167 opportunities**

New opportunities should not be added during qualification unless they emerge through:

- expert responses,
- collaborator introductions,
- network activity,
- qualification research revealing a genuinely important omission,
- or a newly emerging opportunity with unusually high strategic relevance.

New discoveries should be treated as exceptions, not as a reason to reopen broad discovery.

---

## 2.2 Layer-by-Layer Execution

The database must move through the funnel **one complete layer at a time**.

Do not process:

> OPP-001 → verification → ranking → outreach

before OPP-002 has even been structurally reviewed.

Instead:

> all opportunities through L1  
> → freeze L1 result  
> → all survivors through L2  
> → freeze L2 result  
> → continue.

This reduces bias and preserves comparability.

---

## 2.3 Deeper Research Must Become Progressively More Selective

Early layers should be:

- fast,
- standardized,
- evidence-based,
- shallow enough to process the whole universe.

Later layers may involve deep research but only for opportunities that have already earned further investment.

> **The deeper the layer, the fewer opportunities should remain.**

---

## 2.4 No Forced Attrition

There is no target number of opportunities that must survive.

The funnel must measure reality rather than force the database toward a predetermined number.

Possible outcomes are all acceptable:

- 30 qualified opportunities,
- 60 qualified opportunities,
- 100 qualified opportunities.

Quality determines the denominator.

---

## 2.5 No Forced Survival Rate

The objective is **not** to prove that 80% of the original 167 are good.

The 80/20 rule applies only after the qualified actionable universe has been identified.

---

## 2.6 Opportunity-Type-Specific Evaluation

A researcher, dataset, grant, EEG device, fellowship, network, ethics body, and foundation model cannot be evaluated using identical criteria.

Universal funnel logic will be used, but verification and qualification rules must be interpreted according to opportunity type.

---

## 2.7 Evidence Hierarchy

When verifying claims, prefer:

1. official institution / organization source,
2. official programme / product / documentation page,
3. primary peer-reviewed publication,
4. official registry / government source,
5. authoritative research repository,
6. reputable secondary source only when stronger sources are unavailable.

Important qualification decisions should not rely primarily on search snippets, aggregators, or promotional summaries.

---

## 2.8 Preserve Traceability

Every opportunity that is:

- merged,
- removed,
- rejected,
- downgraded,
- or significantly reclassified

must retain:

- original OPP ID,
- reason,
- decision date,
- evidence basis.

No record should silently disappear.

---

## 2.9 Armenian / Diaspora Principle

Armenian and Armenian-diaspora opportunities receive deliberate attention because they may provide:

- institutional access,
- local research support,
- mentorship,
- bridge relationships,
- national funding,
- regional leverage.

They do **not** receive lower scientific or qualification standards.

Global scientific quality remains the primary standard.
