## Purpose

`active_opportunity_execution_ledger.md` answers **what to do with every active opportunity**.

This roadmap answers the more practical question:

> **What should we start with, what should happen in parallel, what must wait for preparation, and what should remain dormant until a trigger appears?**

The roadmap does **not** change L8 rankings or decisions. It reorganizes the same 53 active opportunities by execution urgency, readiness, dependency and ability to unlock later work.

---

# 1. Governing Execution Order

Use this priority logic whenever strategic rank and practical timing conflict:

1. **P0 — Calendar-Critical:** protect deadlines/windows that can disappear.
2. **P1 — Foundation / Unlockers:** create reusable artifacts that unlock many opportunities.
3. **P2 — No-Regret Execute Now:** start technical/resource actions that do not require another person's permission.
4. **P3 — Prepare → Execute Soon:** prepare high-value outbound actions, then execute as soon as their prerequisites are ready.
5. **P4 — Path A Coordinated:** do not duplicate expert contacts; prepare conversion notes and continue in the existing relationship.
6. **P5 — Trigger-Dependent:** preserve active opportunities but do not spend current execution effort until their trigger exists.

> **Calendar urgency overrides normal sequencing, but urgency does not override methodological discipline.**
