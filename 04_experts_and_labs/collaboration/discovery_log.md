Interesting Patterns

The strongest direct-fit landscape clusters around TMR, sleep-memory electrophysiology, closed-loop stimulation, and memory-reactivation decoding.

Several Path A experts also represent genuine Path E collaboration opportunities; this cross-track overlap is scientifically useful rather than duplicative.

A subset of groups already combines sleep neuroscience with engineering-relevant methods such as real-time classification, machine learning, event detection, or closed-loop acoustic stimulation.

Geographic coverage is strongest in North America and Europe, with important direct-fit nodes in Hong Kong, Singapore, and Argentina.

Interesting Patterns

Section 02 naturally separates into three technical layers: sensing, decoding, and closed-loop control.

Several strong opportunities address sparse, ear-based, or wearable EEG, which may become important for later-generation Neuro-TMR even though EEG-based V1 remains the current reference path.

Real-time sleep-state inference is already being studied outside TMR, especially in projects such as ETH Zurich's SleepLoopFM; this creates a valuable capability bridge for Path V.

Bioelectronics groups extend the opportunity universe beyond software and sleep science into future long-duration, low-burden neural interfaces.

The strongest adjacent opportunities are not necessarily sleep-memory groups; their value comes from solving a specific technical bottleneck that Neuro-TMR will eventually face.
