## L3 Interpretation

L3 evaluates whether Neuro-TMR has a plausible mechanism to obtain value from an opportunity.

`L3_ACCESSIBLE_NOW` does not mean:

- guaranteed acceptance,
- guaranteed collaboration,
- free access,
- high probability of success,
- high scientific value,
- or recommendation to act.

It means that a legitimate direct access mechanism currently exists.

`L3_ACCESSIBLE_WITH_BRIDGE` means that the opportunity can plausibly become accessible through an institutional, scientific, consortium, legal-entity, sponsor, host, clinical, or other identifiable bridge.

`L3_CONDITIONAL_TIMING` means that access is primarily dependent on a future application cycle, academic/career stage, technology maturity, project maturity, legal-entity formation, or another time-dependent prerequisite.

Where multiple access constraints exist, the status records the **dominant current access barrier**.
