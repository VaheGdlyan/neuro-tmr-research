# L6 — Strategic Actionability

**Date:** 2026-08-20  
**Input:** 167 L5-characterized qualification units  
**Status:** COMPLETE ✅

## Purpose

L6 determined whether each surviving opportunity justifies further Neuro-TMR investment.

The layer integrated prior funnel evidence rather than evaluating any dimension in isolation.

Inputs included:

- L3 access / eligibility / usability
- L4 contribution relevance
- L5 quality / evidence / distinct value
- current Neuro-TMR project stage
- dependencies
- preparation burden
- timing
- cost / effort
- expected value
- alternative opportunities
- bottleneck contribution
- and existence of a meaningful next action

The core question was:

> **Is this opportunity worth additional Neuro-TMR effort now, after preparation, as a reserve, only after a future trigger, or not at all?**

---

## Research Method

L6 did not repeat deep research across all 167 opportunities.

The existing verified qualification evidence was used as the primary basis.

Additional current-state research was performed selectively when actionability could materially depend on:

- a current application deadline,
- an active funding cycle,
- eligibility,
- technology maturity,
- or another time-sensitive condition.

This preserved bounded research while allowing strategic decisions to reflect present conditions.

## Watch Policy

`L6_WATCH` opportunities are valid opportunities whose current expected value does not justify L7 enrichment.

They remain in a structured watch universe.

Typical watch triggers include:

### Hardware / sensing

- V1 hardware requirements become clearer
- wearable reduction becomes a current bottleneck
- stronger sleep validation appears

### Funding

- new relevant call opens
- institutional host is established
- qualified PI / consortium exists
- eligible prior results exist
- startup or SME stage is reached

### Placements / training

- applicant reaches required academic/career stage
- next programme cycle opens
- appropriate host laboratory becomes available

### Governance

- human participant research begins
- multi-site study begins
- medical-device classification becomes relevant
- U.S.-federally supported research becomes realistic

### Future technology

- evidence quality improves
- Path V reaches the corresponding architecture stage
- multi-site validation becomes necessary

Watch is therefore a reversible deferment, not a rejection.

## Strategic Patterns

### Section 01 — Scientific Collaboration

The direct TMR / sleep-memory laboratories remain strategically strong.

Researchers already involved through Path A were generally classified as:

`L6_ACTIONABLE_WITH_PREPARATION`

because Path E should not immediately duplicate the expert-review interaction.

The preparation step is to convert relevant Path A learning or relationship state into a specific collaboration proposition.

Scientific laboratories not already embedded in the first Path A wave can be directly actionable where a concrete collaboration question exists.

---

### Section 02 — Adjacent Capability

The strongest immediate survivors are capabilities directly connected to:

- EEG analysis,
- real-time sleep decoding,
- wearable-vs-PSG validation,
- sparse-channel sleep sensing,
- and mobile EEG.

Broad future bioelectronics opportunities were moved to WATCH when meaningful value depends on a later hardware-generation question.

General closed-loop / BCI groups with valid but overlapping capability were commonly retained as Reserve Candidates.

---

### Section 03 — Armenian Scientific Ecosystem

The strongest actionable mechanisms are those offering:

- local neuroscience,
- memory-related science,
- EEG,
- neurotechnology,
- PSG,
- sleep infrastructure,
- or language/cognitive experimentation.

Broader engineering and signal-processing structures remain valuable reserves.

Two opportunities were rejected where the contribution was either too peripheral or insufficiently distinct relative to stronger local alternatives.

Armenian opportunities received deliberate attention but were not evaluated under a lower scientific or strategic threshold.

---

### Section 04 — Armenian Diaspora Bridges

This section experienced the strongest strategic attrition.

Direct sleep, EEG, neuroengineering, neuropsychology, and active Armenian brain-science bridges remain qualified.

Several prestigious but only generally connected neuroscience/neurology researchers were rejected because:

> **prestige and Armenian connection alone do not constitute sufficient Neuro-TMR actionability.**

This preserves the project's global scientific-quality standard while retaining the strongest diaspora bridges.

---

### Section 05 — Data / Research Infrastructure

This is the most immediately actionable family.

Public PSG, wearable EEG, arousal, staging, generalization, and infrastructure resources can directly support Path V.

Most therefore remain Actionable.

Only resources whose marginal role overlaps substantially with stronger datasets were held in reserve.

---

### Section 06 — Global Funding

No global funding opportunity was classified as directly Actionable.

This is deliberate.

Most major mechanisms currently require combinations of:

- institutional host,
- qualified PI,
- consortium,
- particular research result,
- open call,
- career stage,
- or technology maturity.

Four strong funding ecosystems remain Reserve Candidates.

Eleven remain Watch opportunities.

Funding quality alone does not make a programme strategically actionable at the current project stage.

---

### Section 07 — Armenian Funding

The Armenian portfolio is more practically useful because several mechanisms could become available through realistic local institutional bridges.

The strongest preparation opportunities involve:

- international-scientist integration,
- laboratory formation,
- equipment,
- international PI-led research groups,
- and focused research grants.

AI Virtual Institute compute support is directly actionable because it can support current Path V computational work.

Company-stage mechanisms remain Watch opportunities until scientific validation and organizational maturity justify them.

---

### Section 08 — Fellowships / Placements / Training

These opportunities remain scientifically valuable.

Most are nevertheless WATCH because academic/career-stage or programme-cycle constraints dominate present actionability.

Three remain Actionable With Preparation:

- FENS / IBRO-PERC Exchange Fellowships
- CAJAL Neurobiology of Sleep
- Armenia Professional Training Programme

These mechanisms can potentially become concrete through eligibility confirmation, host relationships, financing, or institutional affiliation.

---

### Section 09 — Hardware / Industry

The hardware family remains strongly represented but most requires preparation.

Preparation includes:

- defining required EEG channels,
- confirming raw-stream access,
- testing SDK/API constraints,
- clarifying latency,
- determining overnight suitability,
- understanding cost,
- and aligning hardware evaluation with Path V results.

Open or relatively accessible developer ecosystems can be actionable earlier.

More expensive, institutional, or complementary platforms remain reserve candidates.

---

### Section 10 — Networks / Events

Networks survive where they provide meaningful access to:

- sleep researchers,
- EEG experts,
- neurotechnology engineers,
- Armenian brain-health researchers,
- mentorship,
- or downstream opportunities.

Broad overlapping societies remain reserve candidates rather than being rejected because they can still generate valuable second-order opportunities.

No network has yet been ranked.

---

### Section 11 — Governance / Ethics

Most governance mechanisms are not immediate project bottlenecks because current Path V is validation-lite rather than a human intervention study.

Local institutional ethics, research administration, privacy, data management, and registration mechanisms therefore remain mostly Reserve Candidates.

OSF preregistration is directly actionable because transparent methodology can strengthen research work before clinical-stage development.

Medical-device and U.S. multi-site mechanisms remain WATCH until the relevant research context exists.

---

### Section 12 — Future Horizon

Future technologies were evaluated particularly conservatively.

Phase-specific closed-loop TMR remains Actionable because it directly informs a live architecture question.

Sleep and EEG foundation models and spindle-aware closed-loop approaches remain Actionable With Preparation because they can become bounded comparative experiments after core Path V baselines exist.

Reactivation decoding and personalized cue scheduling remain Reserve Candidates.

PPG foundation modelling, integrated in-ear hardware, and federated validation remain WATCH.

The Virtual Brain was rejected because its high scientific quality does not overcome its currently speculative and effort-heavy Neuro-TMR role.

## Interpretation

L6 is the first substantial strategic narrowing of Path E.

The result should not be interpreted as:

> “120 opportunities should now be contacted.”

The 120 are only the opportunities strong enough to justify deeper decision-critical enrichment.

Some are:

- datasets,
- research tools,
- hardware,
- funding,
- laboratories,
- networks,
- governance mechanisms,
- or future technologies.

Their eventual actions will therefore differ.

The next phase must continue to preserve this opportunity-type distinction.
