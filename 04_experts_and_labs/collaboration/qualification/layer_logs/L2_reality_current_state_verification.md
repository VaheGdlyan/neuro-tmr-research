## Purpose

L2 tested whether each structurally valid opportunity from L1:

- currently exists,
- remains active,
- is represented by a current organization / person / programme / product / dataset / network / governance mechanism / technology,
- and is materially described accurately in the discovery database.

L2 did not evaluate:

- accessibility,
- eligibility,
- Neuro-TMR relevance,
- scientific quality,
- strategic value,
- actionability,
- ranking,
- priority,
- or outreach potential.

Those questions remain reserved for later layers.

---

## Verification Method

The complete 168-unit qualification universe was reviewed section by section.

Verification prioritized:

1. official institutional sources,
2. official programme / product documentation,
3. government sources,
4. primary scientific publications,
5. authoritative research repositories,
6. strong secondary evidence only where required to resolve a current-state question.

Older datasets were not treated as obsolete merely because of age.

Future-horizon opportunities were not required to be mature Neuro-TMR technologies; L2 tested only whether the underlying research/model/platform/prototype actually exists and whether the discovery description remained materially accurate.

## Material Changes

### OPP-043 — Mariam Avagyan / sleep-EEG signal-analysis capability

The individual and underlying signal-processing capability remain real.

However, the sleep-EEG framing requires correction.

The REM-sleep EEG and hippocampal EEG work associated with the profile dates to earlier research, while current research activity is focused primarily on mathematical deep learning, signal processing, high-dimensional modelling, and related applications.

The opportunity therefore remains valid, but should be represented as:

> **signal-processing / machine-learning expertise with historical sleep-EEG research experience**

rather than as an active dedicated sleep-EEG research programme.

---

### OPP-048 — Life Sciences International Postgraduate Educational Center / UNESCO Chair in Life Sciences

The institution remains active.

The current formal UNESCO listing identifies the opportunity as the:

> **Network on Research and Postgraduate Education in Biophysics, Biotechnology and Environmental Health**

hosted by the Life Sciences International Postgraduate Educational Center.

Institutional materials continue to use UNESCO Chair in Life Sciences terminology, but the current formal UNESCO designation should be used during qualification.

### OPP-101 — ADVANCE Research Grants

ADVANCE remains active.

Its operating structure has materially changed since its earlier FAST-led form.

From January 2025, the programme's core funding and administration continue under the:

> **Higher Education and Science Committee of the Ministry of Education, Science, Culture and Sports of the Republic of Armenia**

while FAST remains the programme's originator and continues supportive ecosystem, dissemination, and networking functions.

The opportunity remains valid but must be represented using the current administrative structure.
