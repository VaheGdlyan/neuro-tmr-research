# L3 — Access / Eligibility / Usability

**Date:** 2026-08-20  
**Input:** 168 L2-verified qualification units  
**Status:** COMPLETE ✅

## Purpose

L3 evaluated whether Neuro-TMR has a plausible mechanism to obtain value from every verified opportunity.

The central question was:

> **Can Neuro-TMR realistically access, use, apply to, join, request, or otherwise obtain value from this opportunity?**

L3 did not evaluate:

- Neuro-TMR relevance,
- scientific quality,
- evidence strength,
- distinct value,
- strategic actionability,
- expected response probability,
- cost-benefit,
- ranking,
- portfolio priority,
- or outreach strategy.

Those dimensions remain reserved for L4 and later layers.

---

## Status Definitions Applied

### `L3_ACCESSIBLE_NOW`

A legitimate direct current route exists.

Depending on opportunity type, this may mean:

- direct scientific inquiry,
- public download,
- DUA/application-controlled dataset access,
- open-source software/model access,
- purchase,
- SDK/API application,
- research inquiry,
- membership,
- conference registration,
- public institutional guidance,
- or direct programme application.

This status does not imply guaranteed acceptance or free access.

### `L3_ACCESSIBLE_WITH_BRIDGE`

Access requires an identifiable but plausible dependency such as:

- research institution,
- host laboratory,
- qualified PI,
- consortium,
- collaborator,
- institutional affiliation,
- responsible investigator,
- legal entity,
- clinical/research sponsor,
- participating data owner,
- or research partnership.

### `L3_CONDITIONAL_TIMING`

Access is principally dependent on:

- future application cycle,
- academic stage,
- career stage,
- future call,
- technology maturity,
- qualifying previous research result,
- project maturity,
- company formation,
- or future study/product definition.

### `L3_NOT_REALISTICALLY_ACCESSIBLE`

No plausible access pathway exists.

### `L3_UNKNOWN`

Available evidence is insufficient to establish the access pathway.

Where multiple constraints existed, the status reflects the **dominant current access barrier**.

## Scientific Collaboration Access Pattern

Sections 01–04 were treated according to the scientific-collaboration rule defined in the funnel specification.

A laboratory/researcher is `L3_ACCESSIBLE_NOW` when a legitimate current mechanism exists to initiate scientific dialogue, such as:

- institutional email,
- laboratory contact route,
- research inquiry,
- existing Path A relationship,
- or plausible project-based contact.

This does not imply that collaboration will occur.

Response probability and expected collaboration value belong to L6.

---

## Data / Infrastructure Access Pattern

All Section 05 qualification units have identifiable access mechanisms.

These include:

- public downloads,
- research accounts,
- data-use agreements,
- repository access,
- open-source software,
- and ordinary application-controlled research access.

A DUA or account requirement alone was not considered a bridge if the resource exposes a standard direct application mechanism to researchers.

Both L1-derived units remain independently accessible:

- OPP-078A — NEMAR
- OPP-078B — Dreamento

## Funding Access Pattern

Global and Armenian funding opportunities produced the largest concentration of access dependencies.

The principal barriers were:

- institutional applicant requirements,
- consortium requirements,
- investigator career stage,
- specific future calls,
- legal-entity requirements,
- qualifying previous research outputs,
- and technology/company maturity.

Funding prestige was not evaluated.

Only the mechanism by which Neuro-TMR could eventually access the funding was considered.

---

## Fellowship / Placement Access Pattern

Most structured programmes are real and recurrent but are not continuously accessible independent of applicant status.

Common constraints include:

- university enrollment,
- completed academic semesters,
- undergraduate/graduate status,
- host-laboratory approval,
- application windows,
- recommendation requirements,
- institutional affiliation,
- and programme-specific career stage.

These were classified primarily as `L3_CONDITIONAL_TIMING`.

CSHL URP was classified `L3_ACCESSIBLE_WITH_BRIDGE` because international access from a foreign institution depends on an additional institutional route beyond the ordinary application cycle.

Ten of twelve Section 09 opportunities expose a direct route through:

Two were classified as bridge-dependent:

### OPP-132 — Elemind

## Network / Event Access Pattern

All Section 10 opportunities expose a legitimate participation mechanism at the network level.

Depending on the opportunity, this includes:

- membership,
- non-member registration,
- student registration,
- online community,
- chapter participation,
- conference registration,
- direct network contact,
- or professional-community participation.

A particular conference deadline being closed does not make the broader society/community inaccessible when continuing network access exists.

---

## Governance Access Pattern

Governance produced the highest proportion of bridge-dependent opportunities.

This is expected because:

- IRBs operate through institutions,
- research administration operates internally,
- REDCap long-term deployment operates through organizations,
- SMART IRB operates between institutions,
- Federalwide Assurance is institutional,
- clinical-study registration requires an actual responsible study structure,
- and external IRB services do not eliminate applicable local requirements.

Only mechanisms that can be used directly without first establishing a host structure were classified as accessible now.

## Future-Horizon Access Pattern

Most Section 12 resources are directly accessible because they provide:

- open code,

- public research software,

Two require bridges:

### OPP-165 — Integrated In-Ear EEG + Auditory Stimulation Platform

Prototype-level research access requires connection to the research team or an equivalent collaboration route.

### OPP-166 — MLCommons MedPerf

Software access itself is open, but realizing the opportunity's intended multi-institution private-data validation mechanism requires participating institutions and data owners.
