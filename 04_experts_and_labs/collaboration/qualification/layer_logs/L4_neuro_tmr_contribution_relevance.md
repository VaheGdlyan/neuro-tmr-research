# L4 — Neuro-TMR Contribution Relevance

**Date:** 2026-08-20  
**Input:** 168 L3-classified qualification units  
**Status:** COMPLETE ✅

## Purpose

L4 evaluated the concrete contribution each opportunity could make to Neuro-TMR.

The central test was:

> **This opportunity could materially help Neuro-TMR by ________.**

The layer evaluated contribution rather than prestige, accessibility, quality, probability of success, or strategic priority.

---

## Controlled Research Method

The existing verified Opportunity Universe was used as the primary evidence base.

L4 did not repeat full deep research for all 168 opportunities.

Additional external research was used selectively when:

- the contribution was unclear,
- the discovery description appeared broader than the actual capability,
- a weak-versus-none decision required clarification,
- or a future-horizon opportunity required checking whether its proposed contribution was sufficiently concrete.

This maintained bounded research while allowing deeper investigation where a classification could materially change.

## Interpretation

L4 produced the first hard funnel attrition:

**168 → 167**

However, the more important result is the internal distribution:

- 112 opportunities are either Direct High or Strong Adjacent,
- 43 have meaningful but future-dependent relevance,
- 12 retain only weak relevance,
- 1 has no material Neuro-TMR contribution.

This does not mean that 112 opportunities are high quality or should be pursued.

L4 answers only:

> **What could this opportunity contribute?**

L5 will ask the harder question:

> **Is that opportunity actually strong, evidence-supported, and distinct enough to deserve continued attention?**
