# F7 — Governance / Institutional Enablement

## Purpose

Evaluate ethics, IRB, institutional-governance, regulatory, and research-enablement mechanisms required for future human-subject Neuro-TMR validation.

## Main Questions

L7 focused on:

- institutional affiliation requirements;
- responsible-investigator requirements;
- IRB/ethics-review roles;
- study-protocol requirements;
- multi-site research mechanisms;
- regulatory dependencies;
- when each mechanism becomes relevant.

## Main Contribution Types

Candidates in this family may enable:

- ethical human-subject research;
- institutional study approval;
- research governance;
- multi-site collaboration;
- future regulatory compliance.

## L7 Finding

Governance mechanisms cannot be treated as independent collaboration opportunities in the same way as laboratories or datasets.

A future Neuro-TMR human study will generally require:

1. an institutional host;
2. a responsible investigator;
3. a defined protocol;
4. human-subject ethics review;
5. additional regulatory mechanisms where applicable.

Mechanisms such as IRB reliance frameworks or institutional assurances do not replace actual ethical review.

Their relevance is therefore triggered by the creation of a concrete human-subject study.
