# F3 — Funding & Institutional Support

## Purpose

Evaluate funding mechanisms, institutional-support programs, grants, innovation pathways, and Armenian/international research-support opportunities.

## Main Questions

L7 focused on:

- current program status;
- applicant eligibility;
- Armenian participation;
- institutional requirements;
- consortium requirements;
- project maturity requirements;
- application timing;
- funding purpose.

## Main Contribution Types

Funding/support candidates may provide:

- research funding;
- institutional support;
- prototype funding;
- international collaboration funding;
- R&D incentives;
- startup support;
- access to larger research ecosystems.

## L7 Finding

Funding opportunities differ significantly in accessibility.

Some require:

- a university or research institution;
- international consortium participation;
- a registered company;
- a more mature prototype;
- a future application cycle.

Therefore scientific or financial attractiveness does not automatically mean immediate usability.

Most remaining uncertainty is **institution-, eligibility-, or future-cycle-dependent**.
