# F5 — Hardware / Industry / Technology Access

## Purpose

Evaluate EEG companies, wearable platforms, BCI systems, sleep devices, developer ecosystems, and industry partners relevant to a future Neuro-TMR implementation.

## Main Questions

L7 focused on:

- raw EEG availability;
- real-time streaming;
- APIs and SDKs;
- LSL or external software integration;
- EEG channel configuration;
- sampling characteristics;
- research access;
- audio/intervention capability;
- commercial or licensing dependencies.

## Main Contribution Types

Candidates may serve as:

- EEG acquisition platforms;
- wearable EEG systems;
- closed-loop sleep platforms;
- research hardware;
- physiological adjunct sensors;
- future technology partners.

## L7 Finding

A critical distinction emerged between platforms capable of supplying **real-time EEG** and devices that provide only peripheral physiology.

Some systems already expose developer-accessible real-time neural data.

Others appear technically relevant but require direct discussion regarding APIs, research access, pricing, licensing, or integration rights.

Peripheral sensing systems may still be useful for arousal or autonomic monitoring but should not be confused with the V1 EEG core.
