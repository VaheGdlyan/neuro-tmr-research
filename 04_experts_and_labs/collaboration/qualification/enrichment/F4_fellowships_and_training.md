# F4 — Fellowships / Placements / Training

## Purpose

Evaluate research internships, fellowships, neuroscience schools, training programs, placements, and mentored research opportunities.

## Main Questions

L7 examined:

- academic-stage requirements;
- age/education eligibility;
- institutional affiliation;
- application timing;
- research versus training value;
- mentorship availability;
- relevance to Neuro-TMR.

## Main Contribution Types

These opportunities may provide:

- neuroscience training;
- laboratory exposure;
- mentorship;
- research experience;
- international scientific connections;
- computational neuroscience skills;
- access to advanced sleep or EEG methods.

## L7 Finding

Several scientifically strong programs are not immediately accessible because they target late undergraduate, graduate, doctoral, or postdoctoral researchers.

Others remain realistic future opportunities.

Therefore this family's main distinction is between:

- currently usable opportunities;
- future academic-stage opportunities;
- institution-dependent opportunities;
- advanced-research programs.

## L7 Status

**L7_READY_STAGE_DEPENDENT**

Eligibility and timing are sufficiently understood for later ranking.
