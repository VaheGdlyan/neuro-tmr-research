# F1 — Scientific Collaboration & Expertise

## Purpose

Evaluate laboratories, researchers, and scientific groups that could directly strengthen Neuro-TMR through neuroscience expertise, experimental collaboration, methodology, or validation.

## Main Questions

L7 focused on:

- whether relevant research activity is current;
- what distinctive capability each group provides;
- whether the capability addresses a Neuro-TMR bottleneck;
- possible forms of collaboration;
- which uncertainties require direct contact.

## Main Contribution Types

Candidates in this family can potentially contribute through:

- Targeted Memory Reactivation expertise;
- sleep and memory neuroscience;
- EEG and sleep-stage analysis;
- closed-loop stimulation;
- oscillation/event detection;
- wearable neurotechnology;
- experimental sleep studies;
- methodological or statistical guidance.

## L7 Finding

The strongest scientific candidates remain highly relevant because they possess capabilities directly connected to the Neuro-TMR scientific and engineering pathway.

However, questions such as willingness to collaborate, available lab capacity, mentorship, data sharing, or participation in validation cannot reliably be answered through further desk research.

These are therefore treated as **contact-dependent residuals**.
