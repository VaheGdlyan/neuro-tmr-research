# L7 — Opportunity Enrichment

This directory contains the research output from **Layer 7 — Targeted Deep Enrichment** of the Neuro-TMR Path E collaboration funnel.

L7 receives the 120 opportunities that survived the L6 Strategic Actionability gate.

Its purpose is to resolve the remaining **decision-critical unknowns** required before comparative ranking begins.

---

# Core Principle

L7 does not attempt to create exhaustive profiles.

The governing question is:

> **What information is still missing that could materially affect how this opportunity should be evaluated in L8?**

Research stops when additional desk research is unlikely to materially change the later comparative judgment.

A candidate's family determines the type of enrichment questions applied to it.

It does not determine its final priority.

---

# Residual Uncertainty

L7 does not require every uncertainty to be eliminated.

A candidate may still be `L7_READY` if the remaining uncertainty requires:

* direct contact;
* a future program or funding cycle;
* institutional affiliation;
* commercial negotiation;
* a concrete study protocol;
* future technological maturation.

These are dependencies rather than failures of desk research.

---

# L7 Status Vocabulary

### `L7_READY`

Decision-critical desk research is sufficiently complete for L8 comparison.

### `L7_READY_CONTACT_DEPENDENT`

The remaining material uncertainty requires direct communication.

### `L7_READY_STAGE_DEPENDENT`

The opportunity is understood but depends on academic, organizational, or project maturity.

### `L7_READY_FUTURE_CYCLE`

The opportunity is understood but the next usable application/event cycle is in the future.

### `L7_READY_INSTITUTION_DEPENDENT`

Use requires a host organization, research institution, responsible investigator, or consortium.

### `L7_READY_INTEGRATION_DEPENDENT`

Technical relevance is established, but exact API, SDK, licensing, hardware, or commercial integration conditions require direct confirmation.

### `L7_READY_FUTURE_HORIZON`

The opportunity is strategically understood but does not justify changing the first-generation Neuro-TMR pathway.

---

# Relationship to L8

L7 produces evidence.

L8 performs comparison.

Therefore these files must not contain final:

* rankings;
* outreach waves;
* portfolio selections;
* active/reserve assignments;
* 80/20 outreach decisions.

Those belong to subsequent qualification layers.
