# L7 — Targeted Enrichment Workspace

## Purpose

This directory contains decision-critical enrichment for the 120 opportunities
that survived L6 into the Qualified Candidate Universe.

L7 does not perform ranking or prioritization.

Its purpose is to provide sufficient comparable information for L8.

## Governing Rule

> Research the decision-critical unknowns, then stop.

## Starting Universe

- L6_ACTIONABLE: 37
- L6_ACTIONABLE_WITH_PREPARATION: 44
- L6_RESERVE_CANDIDATE: 39

Total: 120

L6_WATCH and L6_REJECT opportunities are not enriched during the current pass.

## Required Output Per Opportunity

- Current State
- Specific Neuro-TMR Contribution
- Access Mechanism
- Key Dependencies
- Timing
- Evidence Anchors
- Decision-Critical Unknown
- Decision Impact of Remaining Uncertainty
- Next Concrete Action
- L7 Status

## L7 Statuses

- L7_ENRICHED
- L7_ENRICHMENT_BLOCKED

## Stopping Rule

Enrichment stops when additional research is unlikely to materially change
the opportunity's later L8 evaluation.

Remaining low-impact uncertainty should be documented rather than researched indefinitely.
