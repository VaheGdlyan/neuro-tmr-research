# F2 — Data / Research Infrastructure & Path V Resources

## Purpose

Evaluate datasets, research infrastructure, software, and computational resources that could support Neuro-TMR Validation-Lite and later technical development.

## Main Questions

L7 examined:

- availability of raw EEG or PSG;
- sleep-stage labels;
- number and quality of recordings;
- access mechanisms;
- licensing restrictions;
- analysis tools;
- relevance to Path V;
- distinct value compared with existing alternatives.

## Main Contribution Types

Resources in this family may support:

- sleep-stage classification;
- EEG preprocessing;
- NREM/N3 detection;
- model benchmarking;
- physiological signal analysis;
- validation of real-time pipelines;
- reproducible experimentation.

## L7 Finding

Multiple resources provide technically useful sleep EEG/PSG data and analysis infrastructure suitable for Validation-Lite.

The important distinction is between resources that provide direct usable data/software and infrastructure whose value depends on institutional or application-based access.

The information required for L8 comparison is now sufficiently resolved.

**L7_READY**
