# F8 — Future-Horizon Strategic Technologies

## Purpose

Evaluate emerging technologies that could influence later generations of Neuro-TMR without unnecessarily expanding the V1 scope.

## Main Questions

L7 examined:

- technological maturity;
- current scientific evidence;
- relevance to existing Neuro-TMR bottlenecks;
- potential integration with sleep EEG;
- whether the technology affects V1 or belongs to later generations.

## Main Technology Directions

This family includes areas such as:

- sleep foundation models;
- advanced neural decoding;
- multimodal physiological models;
- digital twins;
- computational brain models;
- future sensing architectures.

## L7 Finding

Several emerging technologies are scientifically important and may eventually strengthen Neuro-TMR.

Potential future applications include:

- richer sleep representations;
- transfer learning;
- event-level neural decoding;
- individualized modelling;
- improved intervention timing;
- multimodal sleep analysis.

However, none currently provides sufficient reason to replace the first-generation pathway:

> **EEG → real-time sleep-state detection → controlled TMR cue delivery**

These technologies should therefore remain strategic options rather than V1 requirements.

**L7_READY_FUTURE_HORIZON**

The family is sufficiently characterized for L8 without expanding current product scope.
