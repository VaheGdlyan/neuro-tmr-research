# Path E — Opportunity Taxonomy

## 1. Purpose

This document defines what the Neuro-TMR project considers an **opportunity** during Phase II Path E.

Path E exists to discover people, organizations, resources, capabilities, programs, relationships, infrastructure, data, and other mechanisms that could materially improve the scientific development of Neuro-TMR.

The purpose of this taxonomy is to prevent the search from becoming artificially narrow.

The project should not search only for:

- TMR laboratories;
- famous neuroscientists;
- formal collaboration programs;
- direct funding opportunities;
- researchers already working on exactly the same problem.

Many of the most useful opportunities may initially appear indirect.

A laboratory may not study TMR but may possess the exact EEG infrastructure required for validation.

A researcher may not study sleep memory but may specialize in real-time physiological signal processing.

A company may not offer collaboration but may provide research hardware, datasets, SDK access, or introductions.

A foundation may not fund neuroscience specifically but may support young researchers, biomedical innovation, Armenian science, or international scientific exchange.

A local institution may not have world-leading TMR expertise but may provide physical EEG access, supervision, participants, repeated meetings, or a realistic pathway toward empirical work.

Therefore:

> **An opportunity is anything with a plausible mechanism for materially advancing Neuro-TMR.**

The discovery stage should favor **possibility and coverage** over premature exclusion.

---

# 2. Core Opportunity Principle

> **Search broadly. Interpret creatively. Screen rigorously later.**

During discovery, the question is not:

> “Is this definitely useful?”

The question is:

> **“Can I describe a plausible pathway through which this could help the project?”**

If the answer is yes, the opportunity should normally enter the database.
