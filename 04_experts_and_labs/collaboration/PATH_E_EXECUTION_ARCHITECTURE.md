# Path E — Scientific Collaboration Execution Architecture

## 1. Purpose

Path E is the collaboration and opportunity-discovery track of Phase II.

Its purpose is to identify and pursue external opportunities that could materially improve, test, challenge, or enable the Neuro-TMR project.

Path E asks:

> **Who, what, or which opportunity could help answer the scientific and technical questions that cannot be resolved through literature review or independent work alone?**

This includes not only formal scientific collaborators, but also:

- researchers;
- laboratories;
- universities;
- research institutes;
- clinical or sleep-research centers;
- EEG / PSG infrastructure;
- relevant datasets;
- wearable neurotechnology groups;
- methodological expertise;
- grants and research funds;
- fellowships;
- research programs;
- foundations;
- hardware or research-access programs;
- mentorship opportunities;
- Armenian and Armenian-diaspora scientific networks;
- adjacent scientific groups with capabilities relevant to Phase II.

Path E is not limited to opportunities explicitly labeled as "TMR collaboration."

The objective is to map and pursue the broader ecosystem capable of moving the project forward.

---

# 2. Core Principle

> **Search broadly. Screen rigorously. Contact actively.**

Path E should not begin with a narrow list of only the most obvious collaborators.

The discovery stage should intentionally include both:

- highly obvious, direct-fit opportunities;
- less obvious but potentially useful opportunities possessing relevant capabilities.

A group does not need to conduct TMR research directly to be valuable.

For example, a laboratory specializing in:

- real-time EEG classification;
- sleep electrophysiology;
- wearable sensing;
- PSG;
- autonomic physiology;
- closed-loop systems;
- signal processing;

may be highly relevant even without a TMR publication history.

Therefore:

> **Capability relevance is more important than superficial topic similarity.**

---

# 3. Geographic Strategy

Path E should operate globally.

Scientific quality and opportunity value remain the primary criteria.

The strongest opportunities worldwide should be actively pursued regardless of geography.

At the same time, Armenian and Armenian-diaspora opportunities should receive deliberate and substantial attention.

The strategy is:

> **Global excellence first, Armenian accessibility deliberately leveraged, scientific fit always preserved.**

---

## 3.1 Global Opportunities

Search for the strongest relevant opportunities worldwide, including:

- leading TMR laboratories;
- sleep and memory laboratories;
- EEG / PSG laboratories;
- closed-loop sleep stimulation groups;
- wearable neurotechnology groups;
- real-time sleep-state estimation researchers;
- neuroscience institutes;
- universities;
- relevant research programs;
- datasets;
- grants and funding mechanisms.

Global opportunities should not be restricted because they may initially appear difficult to access.

A high-value opportunity should still be pursued when the possible scientific benefit is large.

---

## 3.2 Armenia

Armenian researchers, universities, laboratories, hospitals, sleep centers, engineering groups, and research institutions should form a dedicated high-priority search lane.

Potential advantages include:

- easier direct communication;
- physical proximity;
- repeated interaction;
- possible laboratory access;
- EEG / PSG access;
- mentorship;
- local supervision;
- easier participation in validation work;
- potential support for a young Armenian researcher;
- greater possibility of turning communication into real practical work.

An Armenian opportunity should not be selected solely because it is Armenian.

It must still offer scientifically relevant capability.

However, practical accessibility may make a strong Armenian opportunity more actionable than a more prestigious but inaccessible international opportunity.

---

## 3.3 Armenian Diaspora

Search specifically for Armenian and Armenian-origin researchers working internationally in relevant fields.

Diaspora opportunities may combine:

- strong international scientific infrastructure;
- high-quality expertise;
- possible personal or cultural connection to Armenia;
- willingness to support Armenian scientific development.

Diaspora status should be treated as an opportunity multiplier, not as a substitute for scientific fit.

---

# 4. Opportunity Universe

Path E will begin by building a broad:

> **Opportunity Universe**

There is no strict numerical limit during discovery.

If systematic research identifies 30, 50, 70, or more plausible opportunities, they should be recorded.

The goal at this stage is coverage, not premature elimination.

The Opportunity Universe may contain:

### Scientific Collaboration

- TMR researchers;
- sleep-memory researchers;
- EEG researchers;
- sleep electrophysiology laboratories;
- closed-loop stimulation groups;
- wearable neurotechnology groups;
- real-time signal-processing researchers;
- BCI researchers;
- memory-consolidation researchers;
- computational sleep researchers.

### Infrastructure

- PSG laboratories;
- EEG laboratories;
- sleep clinics with research capability;
- university neuroscience facilities;
- biomedical engineering laboratories;
- wearable EEG platforms;
- experimental sleep facilities.

### Data

- public EEG datasets;
- restricted research datasets;
- wearable EEG datasets;
- simultaneous PSG + wearable datasets;
- multimodal sleep datasets;
- physiological datasets relevant to cue eligibility or arousal.

### Research Support

- scientific mentorship;
- methodological consultation;
- advisory relationships;
- supervision opportunities;
- visiting-research opportunities;
- student research programs;
- summer programs.

### Funding and Enabling Opportunities

- research grants;
- youth-science grants;
- fellowships;
- foundations;
- neuroscience funding programs;
- innovation funds;
- research competitions;
- equipment-support programs;
- hardware research programs.

### Adjacent Opportunities

Include groups that may initially appear indirectly related but possess capabilities needed by Phase II.

Examples:

- respiration and autonomic physiology;
- PPG;
- arousal detection;
- real-time biosignal processing;
- embedded physiological systems;
- wearable sensor validation;
- biomedical signal quality;
- causal time-series classification.

---

# 5. Capability-First Search

Every opportunity should be evaluated by asking:

> **What could this opportunity actually provide to Neuro-TMR?**

Possible capability tags:

- TMR expertise
- sleep-memory expertise
- EEG expertise
- PSG expertise
- closed-loop methodology
- sleep-stage estimation
- real-time signal processing
- wearable EEG
- peripheral sensing
- physiological arousal monitoring
- dataset access
- laboratory infrastructure
- participant access
- experimental capability
- validation partnership
- methodological supervision
- scientific mentorship
- hardware access
- grant / funding
- fellowship / program
- institutional support
- introduction to another researcher

Multiple capability tags may be assigned to one opportunity.
