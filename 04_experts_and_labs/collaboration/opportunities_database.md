# Path E — Opportunity Universe

## Purpose

This database is the central discovery and tracking document for all plausible
external opportunities identified during Phase II — Path E.

Path E is not restricted to formal collaboration offers.

An opportunity may be:

- a researcher;
- a laboratory;
- an institution;
- a dataset;
- scientific infrastructure;
- a funding mechanism;
- a fellowship;
- a research program;
- hardware access;
- methodological expertise;
- a scientific network;
- a referral;
- a mentorship relationship;
- an opportunity that already exists;
- or an opportunity that could be proactively created through collaboration.

The objective is to identify:

> **every scientifically plausible mechanism that could materially improve,
> enable, challenge, validate, or accelerate the Neuro-TMR project.**

Inclusion in this database does **not** mean that an opportunity will
automatically be contacted or pursued.

The working principle is:

> **Discover broadly.  
> Understand the mechanism of value.  
> Screen rigorously later.  
> Contact actively once qualified.**

---

# Database Rules

## Rule 1 — Broad Inclusion During Discovery

During discovery, bias toward inclusion.

An opportunity should normally be recorded when:

1. it is real;
2. a plausible connection to Neuro-TMR exists;
3. the possible mechanism of value can be described.

The opportunity does not need to be obviously related to TMR.

---

## Rule 2 — One Canonical Entry

Every opportunity should have only one primary database entry.

If an opportunity belongs to several categories:

- select one **Primary Section**;
- represent the other relationships through tags and cross-references.

Do not duplicate the same opportunity across multiple sections.

---

## Rule 3 — Capability Before Prestige

An opportunity should not be prioritized merely because the institution or
researcher is famous.

The important question is:

> **What can this opportunity actually contribute to Neuro-TMR?**

---

## Rule 4 — Existing and Created Opportunities Both Count

Path E searches for two forms of opportunity:

### Existing Opportunity

Something already available:

- active collaboration program;
- dataset;
- grant;
- fellowship;
- lab project;
- research placement;
- hardware program.

### Created Opportunity

A collaboration that does not currently exist but could be proposed.

Example:

> A neuroscience or EEG laboratory does not currently study TMR, but its
> capabilities could support a scientifically meaningful joint TMR
> investigation.

Both forms belong in Path E.
