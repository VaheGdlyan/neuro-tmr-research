Path E — Opportunity Universe

Purpose

This document is the master operational database for opportunities discovered
during Phase II — Path E.

It records people, laboratories, institutions, datasets, infrastructure,
programs, funding mechanisms, hardware access, networks, and other external
mechanisms that may materially advance Neuro-TMR.

The rules governing what counts as an opportunity are defined in:

OPPORTUNITY_TAXONOMY.md

The execution strategy for discovery, screening, qualification, outreach,
and collaboration is defined in:

PATH_E_EXECUTION_ARCHITECTURE.md

This database answers a different question:

What opportunities actually exist?

Inclusion in this database does not imply endorsement, qualification,
or intent to contact.

During discovery:

Bias toward inclusion.

During qualification:

Bias toward evidence.

During outreach:

Bias toward relevance and personalization.
