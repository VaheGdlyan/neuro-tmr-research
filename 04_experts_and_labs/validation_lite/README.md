# Validation-Lite — Track V

This folder supports **Track V — Preliminary Technical Validation** of Phase II.

The goal is to gain practical experience with real sleep EEG data and investigate technical prerequisites relevant to the Phase-I reference pathway.

The initial direction is to work with **publicly available sleep EEG datasets** and study sleep-state classification, with particular attention to:

* sleep-stage discrimination;
* reduced-channel EEG;
* subject-to-subject variability;
* classification uncertainty;
* failure modes;
* causal / real-time constraints.

This work is **not validation of a complete TMR system** and is not product engineering.

Its purpose is to test assumptions, understand practical limitations, and generate better technical questions for expert review and future collaboration.

Findings from this track will feed back into Tracks A and E and eventually into the Phase-II synthesis.
