## Why This Expert Matters

Hong-Viet Ngo-Dehning is one of the most directly relevant experts for evaluating the closed-loop component of Neuro-TMR.

His work helped establish phase-locked auditory stimulation of sleep slow oscillations and later directly applied slow-oscillation closed-loop control to TMR.

He is therefore particularly valuable for determining whether our stage-aware first-generation pathway is a defensible simplification or whether event/phase-level timing needs to be considered earlier.

## Relevant Research

### Foundational Work
- **Ngo et al. (2013) — "Auditory Closed-Loop Stimulation of the Sleep Slow Oscillation Enhances Memory" — Neuron**
  - Demonstrated real-time phase-locked auditory stimulation of endogenous slow oscillations and associated memory benefits.
  - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2013.03.006

- **Ngo-Dehning & Staresina (2022) — "Shaping Overnight Consolidation via Slow-Oscillation Closed-Loop Targeted Memory Reactivation" — PNAS**
  - Directly tested phase-specific closed-loop TMR.
  - Highly relevant to our stage-aware vs. phase-aware decision.
  - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2123428119

### Recent Work
- **Esfahani et al. (2023) — "Closed-loop auditory stimulation of sleep slow oscillations: Basic principles and best practices"**
  - Practical methodological guidance for real-time closed-loop sleep stimulation.

- **Dudysová et al. (2024) — "Closed-loop auditory stimulation of slow-wave sleep in chronic insomnia: a pilot study"**
  - Extends closed-loop sleep stimulation into a clinical sleep context.
