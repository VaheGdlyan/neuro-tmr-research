## Relevant Research

### Foundational Work
- **Diekelmann et al. (2012) — "Offline consolidation of memory varies with time in slow wave sleep and can be accelerated by cuing memory reactivations"**
  - Direct evidence that cue-triggered reactivation can influence consolidation during SWS.
  - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nlm.2012.07.002

### Recent Work
- **Carbone & Diekelmann (2024) — "An update on recent advances in targeted memory reactivation during sleep" — npj Science of Learning**
  - Current overview of TMR mechanisms, methodological issues, applications, and limitations.
  - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-024-00244-8

- **Carbone et al. (2024) — "Comparing targeted memory reactivation during slow wave sleep and sleep stage 2"**
  - Found no significant behavioral superiority of SWS over N2 in the tested paradigm.
  - DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-59696-y
