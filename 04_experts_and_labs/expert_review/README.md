# External Expert Review — Track A

This folder supports **Track A — External Expert Review** of Phase II.

The goal is to expose the conclusions of Phase I to researchers with deeper expertise in relevant areas and actively search for weaknesses in the current reasoning.

Expert review will focus on:

* scientific criticism;
* missing or overlooked evidence;
* challenged assumptions;
* alternative interpretations;
* methodological concerns;
* recommendations for further research or validation.

The objective is **not to find experts who agree with the project**.

The objective is to determine where the current EEG-guided, stage-aware, closed-loop TMR reference pathway may be wrong, incomplete, overstated, or worth testing further.

All substantial feedback will later contribute to the Phase-II synthesis and decision process.
