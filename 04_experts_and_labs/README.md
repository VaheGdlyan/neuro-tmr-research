# Experts, Labs & Phase II

This directory represents **Phase II of the Neuro-TMR project**.

Phase I — literature research, research questions, evidence synthesis, and manuscript preparation — is complete.

The purpose of Phase II is to move beyond internal research and begin **external scientific evaluation and preliminary validation** of the conclusions developed during Phase I.

## Current Direction

Phase II will progress through three parallel tracks:

### 1. External Expert Review — Path A

Share the Phase I research and manuscript with researchers in relevant fields and actively seek:

- scientific criticism
- missing evidence
- challenged assumptions
- alternative interpretations
- recommendations for further validation

The goal is not to find agreement, but to determine where the current research pathway may be wrong, incomplete, or worth testing.

### 2. Scientific Collaboration — Path E

Identify and approach relevant researchers and laboratories working in areas such as:

- Targeted Memory Reactivation
- sleep and memory neuroscience
- EEG and sleep electrophysiology
- closed-loop neuroscience
- wearable sleep monitoring

The goal is to explore opportunities for expert guidance, scientific collaboration, and eventually prospective validation.

### 3. Preliminary Technical Validation

While external review and outreach are ongoing, begin working with **publicly available sleep EEG datasets** to study and reproduce sleep-stage classification.

This work is not intended to validate the complete TMR system or begin product engineering.

Its purpose is to gain practical experience with real sleep EEG data and investigate one important prerequisite of the proposed pathway:

> Can sleep state be estimated from EEG in a way that could eventually support real-time closed-loop intervention?

---

## Phase II Principle

The current EEG-guided, stage-aware, closed-loop TMR pathway remains an **evidence-derived hypothesis, not a validated architecture**.

Phase II exists to challenge that hypothesis through:

**Expert Review + Collaboration + Preliminary Validation**

before any serious engineering begins.
